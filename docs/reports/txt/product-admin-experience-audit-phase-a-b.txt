Date: 2026-09-30
Role: CTO + CPO audit. Audit and product decisions only.

EVIDENCE BASE AND LIMITS
------------------------
Read directly from the repository at main @ bc8eaa1. Its application code is
identical to the approved UI baseline 76c206e (bc8eaa1 only adds .gitignore and
lint config). The Phase 2C technical lineage (redesign/phase-2c*) was NOT
audited here. Files that exist in this baseline but are not wired into any
public page, such as components/public/social-content-strip.tsx and
homepage-social-section.tsx, are noted as DORMANT.

Also checked: the live production HTML of /, /about and /articles (read-only
HTTP GET), to see which CMS-driven sections actually render today.

Not verified (marked NOT VERIFIED in the body): actual CMS row contents in
Supabase (I did not query the database), real admin click counts (nothing was
run in a browser), admin behavior on phones, and any analytics.

Tags used:  KEEP / CHANGE / REMOVE / DEFER / NEEDS OWNER DECISION

Headline findings (details below):
 1. The public site is structurally sound and already WhatsApp-first. Most
    problems are copy/claims and a few misplaced features, not architecture.
 2. Several public claims are unsupported ("setiap unit telah melalui inspeksi
    internal", "Kualitas Terjamin", "Merancang ulang standar kemewahan",
    "premium", "kurasi", "layanan purna jual yang eksklusif"). The code's own
    rule is to avoid inventing things; these are invented and need owner
    confirmation or removal.
 3. The admin is further along than it feels. Slug, URL history, stock number
    and media type are already automatic, and SEO fields were already removed
    from the vehicle form. The real friction is: no one-tap status change, no
    sold date, vehicle-form field overload, a mock-era Dashboard and Leads page,
    a placeholder Settings page, and an 8-item sidebar with a duplicated
    content concept.
 4. The biggest Operating System risk is that there is no status-change
    history or sold date in the CMS schema, and the vehicle row mixes
    marketing fields with operational fields.

A1. HOMEPAGE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AREA: Homepage (app/(public)/page.tsx)
CURRENT PURPOSE: Hero slideshow (up to 3 CMS slides, hardcoded fallback) →
  Featured Stock (up to 4) → optional About → Why Perkasa (3 cards) → optional
  Testimonials → generic WhatsApp CTA.
  Live today (read from production HTML): Hero, Featured Stock, Why Perkasa
  (default text), final CTA. The About and Testimonials sections are NOT
  rendering, so they are off or empty in the CMS.
CUSTOMER VALUE: Featured stock and the hero link to /cars are genuinely useful.
  Why Perkasa adds little; it is generic.
BUSINESS VALUE: Featured Stock drives discovery. Final CTA supports conversion.
FRICTION:
  - "Featured Stock" is a manual flag (is_featured). Owner must remember to tick
    it, and a SOLD unit can still be featured.
  - Featured Stock shows "Lihat Semua" only at md+ (hidden on phones), so
    mobile users have no inline path to the full catalogue except the nav.
  - Why Perkasa cards make unsupported claims (see Trust Audit).
  - Hero default copy: "A curated showroom of inspected, premium vehicles —
    every unit verified before it reaches you." Unsupported (English and
    unsupported).
RISK: Testimonials CMS exists; if someone fills it with unverifiable quotes,
  it becomes a trust liability. Today it is empty and correctly hidden.
DECISION: CHANGE (simplify).
RATIONALE: Keep the structure that works (Hero, Stock, CTA). Drop or rewrite
  what makes claims. Do not add sections to look bigger.
RECOMMENDED FUTURE STATE (ideal hierarchy, top to bottom):
  1. Hero: one or two real photos of real current units. One primary action:
     "Lihat Unit Tersedia". One secondary: WhatsApp.
  2. Unit Tersedia: auto-filled with the latest AVAILABLE units (no manual
     "featured" flag needed; see A2). Show "Lihat semua" on all breakpoints.
  3. Cara Pembelian: 4-step compact strip (see A9). This replaces "Why
     Perkasa". It explains the business through process rather than adjectives.
  4. Sudah Terjual (recent, max 3): social proof from real SOLD units. The
     data and card variant already exist (soldPresentation).
  5. Final WhatsApp CTA.
  Optional and only if the owner supplies real content: short About blurb
  (3 lines + link to /about), Testimonials (only real, attributable ones).
  Articles: not on the homepage until there are at least 3 published.
  Section-by-section verdicts:
    Hero ........................ KEEP (simplify to 1–2 slides in practice)
    Available / Featured Units .. KEEP (make automatic)
    About ....................... DEFER (homepage block off; /about is enough)
    Why Perkasa ................. REMOVE from homepage (replace with How to Buy)
    Testimonials ................ DEFER until real, attributable quotes exist
    How to Buy .................. ADD (new, compact)
    Financing guidance .......... DEFER on homepage (link from vehicle detail)
    SOLD / Recent ............... ADD (3 most recent SOLD units)
    Articles .................... DEFER
    Final WhatsApp CTA .......... KEEP

A2. CARS CATALOGUE / MOTORCYCLE CATALOGUE / INVENTORY PRESENTATION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AREA: Cars and Motorcycles catalogues (vehicle-catalogue.tsx, cars/page.tsx,
  motorcycles/page.tsx, lib/data/vehicles.ts)
CURRENT PURPOSE: A grid of vehicles per type. Cars page splits "Unit Tersedia"
  from "Sudah Terjual" (separateSoldInventory). Motorcycles page does NOT use
  that split; it shows one mixed grid.
  Sort order: AVAILABLE, then RESERVED, then SOLD, by tier. Page size 10.
  No filters. No search (the old decorative search icon was already removed).
CUSTOMER VALUE: Good. A small inventory reads as deliberate. The sold archive on

BUSINESS VALUE: Discovery and social proof.
FRICTION:

    That is an inconsistency, not a choice.
  - Cars/Motorcycles catalogue subtitle: "Setiap unit telah melalui kurasi dan

  - Card shows only year, transmission, mileage, price. Fuel and brand are not

  - Pagination is at 10 per page and applies across tiers; with the sold split,
    a page can show "available" units plus part of the sold archive. Fine today,
    worth watching only when inventory passes ~10.
  - Empty state is plain English ("No vehicles available in this category right
    now.") on an otherwise Indonesian site.
  - Reserved units: the "available" section heading says "Unit Tersedia" but
    RESERVED units sit inside it (filter is status !== SOLD). Acceptable, but
    RESERVED should be visually obvious (badge exists).
RISK: LOW. Main risk is scope creep into marketplace filtering.

RATIONALE: The structure is correct. Unify the two catalogues.
RECOMMENDED FUTURE STATE:
  - Apply the Available / Reserved-in-available / Sold split to Motorcycles too.
  - Keep Cars and Motorcycles as separate pages (customers think "mobil" or

  - Translate the empty states to Indonesian and make them useful: "Belum ada
    unit. Tanyakan unit yang akan datang lewat WhatsApp."
  - Filters NOW: none. Optional "Semua / Mobil / Motor" is not needed either
    since they are separate pages.
  - Filters DEFERRED until the available count regularly exceeds ~12 per type:
    (1) brand, (2) price range, (3) transmission, (4) year. Do NOT add: body
    type, fuel, color, location, condition (single showroom, used cars).
  - Sorting NOW: keep the tiered default. Sort control DEFERRED.
  - Pagination: KEEP as is.

A3. VEHICLE DETAIL
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AREA: Vehicle detail (vehicle-detail.tsx, cars/[slug], motorcycles/[slug])
CURRENT PURPOSE: Gallery (lightbox) + title, stock number, price, highlights
  chips, spec grid (year, km, transmission, fuel, exterior color, condition,
  CC, plate), long description, WhatsApp CTA, related vehicles.
CUSTOMER VALUE: HIGH. It already answers most of the five buyer questions.
BUSINESS VALUE: HIGH. It is the main conversion page.
FRICTION / REDUNDANCY:
  - Stock number shown as a large sub-heading under the title; it is an
    internal reference. Buyers do not need it (but the owner can use it in
    WhatsApp chats). Demote it to small text.
  - "Kondisi: Used/New" on a used-car dealer is noise for nearly every unit.
    "Warna Eksterior", "Kapasitas CC" and "Plat Nomor" show "—" when empty,

  - CC is meaningful for motorcycles, less for cars. Plate is useful (buyers do
    check the plate area/expiry) but free-text unverified.
  - Mileage/plate/year duplicate what is also in the highlights chips if the

    where inconsistent or exaggerated claims will appear.
  - Description (vehicle.description): the vehicle form no longer has an input

    an empty description. NOT VERIFIED which live vehicles have description
    text. Decide: either a short optional "Catatan" field or drop the paragraph.
  - Related vehicles heading is English ("Related Vehicles"). It also shows SOLD
    units as "related" purchase options; check this is intended.
  - Missing trust information that buyers ask: tax (pajak) validity, number of
    previous owners, documents (BPKB/STNK) status, service history, and
    honest accident/repair disclosure. NONE of these exist in the schema. These
    are what a used-vehicle buyer wants, but the owner must be able to state
    them truthfully for each unit. See Needs Owner Decision.
  - Financing: no link from a vehicle to an estimate. The buyer has to leave
    and retype the price in /financing.
RISK: MEDIUM. Empty/"—" rows and missing facts look like hidden information.
DECISION: CHANGE (small).
RECOMMENDED FUTURE STATE (answers the five questions):
  WHAT IS IT?     Title, year, cover photo, gallery.
  CONDITION/SPEC  Mileage, transmission, fuel, color, plate (+ CC for motor).
                  Only rows with values. Highlights as max 3–5 short facts.
  HOW MUCH?       Price, prominent. Optional single line "Estimasi cicilan
                  mulai dari Rp X/bulan" only if financing is kept (see A4).
  AVAILABLE?      Status badge (Tersedia / Dipesan / Terjual), already there.
  CONTACT?        One WhatsApp button with pre-filled unit context (already
                  there). Sticky on mobile (DEFER; visual polish).
  Related: show only AVAILABLE units.
  Add (if owner can state them truthfully): pajak berlaku s/d, jumlah pemilik,
  kelengkapan dokumen. These are the highest-value trust additions in this
  whole audit; they are OWNER DECISIONS, not code decisions.

A4. FINANCING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AREA: Financing (financing/page.tsx, financing-calculator.tsx)
CURRENT PURPOSE: Client-side annuity calculator (price input default Rp 500 juta,
  DP slider 10–70%, tenor 12–60 months, interest rate default 6.5% p.a.),
  plus "Tanya Simulasi via WhatsApp" that appends the estimate to the message.
CUSTOMER VALUE: Moderate. People like a rough number.
BUSINESS VALUE: Moderate, but with real risk.
FRICTION / RISK:
  - Nav label and page title say "Simulasi Kredit" and heading "Hitung estimasi
    cicilan Anda" — "simulation" implies something more definite than it is.
  - Default price Rp 500 juta does not relate to inventory (units are Rp 13–95
    juta on the live site). The first thing a visitor sees is an irrelevant
    number.
  - The 6.5% default rate is a made-up flat figure. In Indonesia multi-finance
    rates, admin fees, insurance, and effective-vs-flat interest differ widely
    and are often much higher for used vehicles. A 6.5% default UNDERSTATES
    typical used-vehicle financing cost and can create a false expectation.
  - The disclaimer is tiny (12px, muted) and says "estimasi ini bersifat
    indikatif … belum terhubung ke pengajuan kredit sungguhan." It does not say
    that actual terms depend on the leasing company and applicant profile.
  - WhatsApp message embeds "estimasi cicilan sekitar Rp X" in the customer's
    first message, so the owner may be held to that number.
DECISION: CHANGE (rename, reframe, reduce) — do not build a richer calculator.
RATIONALE: Perkasa cannot guarantee leasing terms, so the feature must read as
  a rough estimate and a conversation starter, never a quote.
RECOMMENDED FUTURE STATE (simplest useful):
  - Keep ONE page, rename to "Estimasi Pembiayaan" (nav: "Estimasi Kredit").
  - Keep the calculator but: default price = 0/empty or a selectable unit;
    default rate left blank or a clearly labeled placeholder the visitor must
    set; show a prominent 4-bullet notice above the result: (1) angka hanya
    estimasi, (2) bunga/DP/tenor/biaya ditentukan perusahaan pembiayaan,
    (3) persetujuan tergantung profil pemohon, (4) simulasi resmi lewat
    WhatsApp.
  - Accept ?price= so a vehicle's detail page can link "Estimasi cicilan unit
    ini" into it pre-filled. This is a link, not a new module.
  - Remove the estimated number from the pre-filled WhatsApp text (say "Saya
    ingin simulasi kredit untuk unit X", not a figure).
  - Do NOT add a separate financing module to the vehicle page (too heavy).
  - If the owner prefers zero risk: the fallback is REMOVE the calculator and
    keep only a "Tanya Simulasi Kredit" WhatsApp CTA on the vehicle page.
    That is acceptable and simpler. NEEDS OWNER DECISION.

Classification: SUPPORTED / UNSUPPORTED / TOO STRONG / REDUNDANT.
"Unsupported" means nothing in the repo or the live data demonstrates it; it
may still be true, in which case the owner should confirm and ideally add
evidence.

 1. "Setiap unit telah melalui kurasi dan inspeksi internal Perkasa Motors."
    (Cars/Motorcycles catalogue headers) ........ UNSUPPORTED, TOO STRONG
 2. "Setiap unit melewati inspeksi menyeluruh untuk memastikan performa dan
    kondisi sempurna." (Why Perkasa card) ........ UNSUPPORTED, TOO STRONG
    ("kondisi sempurna" is a perfection claim on used vehicles.)
 3. "Kualitas Terjamin" (card title) ............. TOO STRONG (implies warranty)
 4. "Terpercaya … Reputasi dalam menghadirkan kendaraan premium berkualitas
    tinggi" ..................................... UNSUPPORTED, REDUNDANT
 5. "Layanan Premium … purna jual yang eksklusif" . UNSUPPORTED (no after-sales
    service is described anywhere)
 6. "A curated showroom of inspected, premium vehicles — every unit verified
    before it reaches you." (hero fallback text) ... UNSUPPORTED, TOO STRONG
 7. "Merancang ulang standar kemewahan" / "standar inspeksi internal" (live
    About) ....................................... UNSUPPORTED, TOO STRONG
 8. "Etalase otomotif digital" (live About) ..... CONFUSING: a physical address
    exists in the footer.
 9. Product descriptions in SEO default: "Curated and inspected by Perkasa
    Motors." (vehicle metadata fallback) ........ UNSUPPORTED
10. Hero slides (live CMS) "Find Your Next Drive." and body "Pilihan kendaraan
    berkualitas dengan informasi yang jelas dan proses pembelian yang mudah."
    ........................................... SUPPORTED (modest, aspirational)
11. Prices, year, mileage, transmission, plate on cards ... SUPPORTED (it is
    the data; accuracy depends on owner entry)
12. SOLD archive ............................. SUPPORTED (factual status)
13. WhatsApp number, address (Cibubur, Jakarta Timur) ... SUPPORTED, assuming
    correct
14. "Premium" used as a general brand word (catalogue meta descriptions,
    Why Perkasa) .............................. TOO STRONG for mainstream used
    vehicles such as the Hyundai Grand Avega listings seen live.
15. Testimonials: none live ................... SUPPORTED (correctly absent)
16. Financing default rate 6.5% ............... MISLEADING DEFAULT (not a claim
    but implied terms; see Financing)

Recommended evidence-based replacements (owner to confirm facts):
  - Real photos with consistent angles; clear status; plate and pajak info.
  - Sold units with month/year.
  - Short factual process (Cara Pembelian).
  - Social links that lead to real content (Instagram, LinkedIn only if real).
  - A plain statement of what is actually checked on each unit, only if a
    checklist really exists (NEEDS OWNER DECISION). Otherwise say nothing
    about inspection.

Duplication risks found in the current CMS:
  1. Price and status are editable by hand in the CMS. When the OS arrives,
     two systems can disagree. Mitigation: a single "source" marker per
     field (MANUAL vs OS) later; for now, keep the fields few and named
     consistently.
  2. No external ID column on vehicles to link an OS record. Without it,
     sync must guess using stock_number. Mitigation (later): add a nullable
     external_id.
  3. No status-change history or sold_at. The OS will want to push a status
     and the timestamp; the CMS cannot store the timestamp.
  4. vehicles.is_published + vehicles.status are two sources of visibility.
     Collapse to one rule.
  5. `leads` table exists in the CMS schema; OS will own CRM.
  6. Duplicate contact/social fields (General vs Footer) create two public
     sources of truth inside the CMS itself.
  7. stock_number generation and its permanent-lock/reuse rules are
     CMS-side business logic (triggers) that the OS may also want to own.
  8. `condition`, `location` fields on vehicles are dealer-internal facts
     that belong to the OS long term.
Overall readiness: MEDIUM. The vehicle table is clean, slugs/URL history and
stock numbers are robust, and the actions layer is separated from data
access. The schema needs three small additive things before any sync: sold
date / status timestamp, external id, and a single publish rule. These are
recommendations only; nothing is changed now.
